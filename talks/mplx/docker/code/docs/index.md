# Shelf

Shelf is the reading list of our book club. Sign in with the name and
password an administrator gave you.

- **Add a book:** fill in title and author under the list and press Add.
- **Mark it as read:** press "Mark as read" next to the book. Unread books
  are listed first.
- **Sign out:** the button next to your name at the top.

Sessions last eight hours of inactivity, and end when the app restarts.
